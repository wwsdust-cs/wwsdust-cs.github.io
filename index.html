About me

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Artificial Intelligence, College of Computer Science and Engineering, at Shandong University of Science and Technology.

I received my Ph.D. from the School of Information Science and Engineering at Shandong University in June 2025, advised by Prof. Yujun Li and Prof. Xianye Ben. From 2023 to 2024, I was a visiting scholar at the Graduate School of Information Science and Electrical Engineering (ISEE), Kyushu University, Japan. Previously, I received my master's degree from the College of Computer Science and Engineering at Shandong University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Jianli Zhao.

My research interests include recommender systems, representation learning, and data mining.
